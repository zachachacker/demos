02 — How a project runs

Simple to hire. Serious on set.

STEP 1

Tell us what you’re making

Send the idea in a message — a launch, an event, a space, a film you can picture but can’t shoot. You get a straight answer on whether it’s a fit and what it would take.

STEP 2

It gets planned properly

Every project gets a treatment and a shot list before anyone touches a camera. You know what’s being made before the shoot day, not after.

STEP 3

You get the film

Delivered cut and graded, in the formats you’ll actually use — widescreen for the big screen, vertical for socials.

Every project is quoted on scope. Tell us what you’re making and you get a number for that job — not a rate card.
